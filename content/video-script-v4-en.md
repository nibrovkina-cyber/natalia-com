# Video Script v4-EN — "Quality service. No leads. Here's the fix."

**Length:** 10:00 (matches RU v4)
**Format:** screen-share 90% + talking head 10%
**Audience:** ONE — solo agency owner, indie marketing freelancer, OR small clinic/salon/SaaS founder doing their own marketing
**Main viral hook:** drag-slider before/after (silent first 5 seconds) + Hopkins-callout without past-business claims

> ⚠️ This is the **EN adaptation** of `video-script-v4.md` (RU original). All findings from 4 audit rounds applied:
> - No em-dashes in spoken lines (conversational EN, not academic)
> - All numbers labeled "demo case" or "market range," not presented as real
> - Block 6 reframed as "what YOU can do" — not personal revenue claim (Natalia is pre-revenue)
> - Pre-revenue admission stated explicitly in body
> - Single central focus: Copy agent through OSH methodology
> - 16 vs 21 clarified: "16 functional agents in tool + 5 methodology foundations = 21 SKILL.md in open-source repo"
> - CTA via comments without commitment to next video ("top 3 phrases I'll break down in a reply, not next video")

## NOTES FOR NATALIA

1. **Speech tempo:** EN audience tolerates ~7 wpm (slower than RU 6-7 wpm). For viewers whose first language isn't English, slower is better.

2. **Accent:** Natalia's natural accent is part of the brand. Don't try to "neutralize" it. Pieter Levels (Dutch accent), Liam Ottley (Australian) — accent is a feature, not a bug.

3. **Cultural translation:** "качественные услуги" → "quality service" works. "индивидуальный подход" → "personalized service" or "tailored experience." Both are equally vapid in their respective languages, that's the point.

4. **Pricing format:** Use $30, not $29.99 (US convention is $29 or $30 for indie SaaS, not $29.99 e-commerce).

5. **Methodology citations:** Hopkins and Ogilvy worked in English. Schwartz was based in NYC. Their original works ARE in English. Use that authority as a strength, not weakness.

6. **Link the RU version:** in description, "Russian version of this video: [link]" — bilingual creators get more reach because the algorithm sees engagement from two language clusters.
